Chief Executive Officer

Osatohamen Emmanuel Clement

Visionary strategic leadership.

EC

Osatohamen Emmanuel Clement is the visionary founder and Chief Executive Officer of ImmanuelPavilion Services Limited, a dynamic, multi-sector enterprise with operations spanning real estate, car dealership, facility management, and general merchandise.

A passionate entrepreneur and strategic thinker, Emmanuel is driven by a deep commitment to delivering exceptional customer service and meeting the evolving needs of individuals and businesses. With a fearless approach to business and a strong belief in taking action, Emmanuel leads with purpose and vision.

He is focused on creating real value and ensuring every client interaction results in a positive experience. His leadership philosophy centers around integrity, responsiveness, and the relentless pursuit of excellence.

Admin Manager

Oyakhilome Francis Kingsley

Operational coordination focus.

FK

Oyakhilome Francis Kingsley serves as the Admin Manager for ImmanuelPavilion, where he plays a vital role in ensuring smooth coordination and communication across our marketing and advertising activities.

Oyakhilome brings a strong background in leadership and digital creativity. He is an active member of Junior Chamber International Nigeria (JCIN) University of Benin, Nigeria (UNIBEN) and currently serving as a Family Head. He has held key roles such as Training Manager and Event Moderator. He also functions as the Public Relations Officer for Watchman Catholic Charismatic Campus Fellowship (WCCCF) UNIBEN.

Beyond his organizational skills, Oyakhilome is a skilled graphic designer and video editor with experience in creating content for events, digital campaigns, and real estate promotions. His energy, discipline, and creative input continue to add value to our team and clients.
